The 3-Step Blast System

1High-Pressure Hot Wash

We hook your empty bins up to our mobile curbside wash system. Our system blasts the interior with scalding hot water at extreme high pressure, scouring away stuck-on grime, trash residue, and build-up that standard garden hoses can't touch.

2Sanitize & Kill Bacteria

Dirty trash cans are breeding grounds for harmful bacteria and maggots. We thoroughly disinfect the entire bin, killing 99.9% of biological germs, mold, and viruses lurking inside and out.

3Deodorize & Freshen

Finally, we wipe down the exterior touchpoints, dry the bins, and apply a premium deodorizing spray that completely eliminates foul odors and leaves behind a crisp, clean scent.
