Our 4-Step Cleaning System

1On-Site Setup & Eco-Wash

We begin right at your curb using your convenient on-site outdoor spigot. We apply a heavy-duty, highly effective all-purpose cleaning solution designed to immediately break down tough grime, grease, and built-up residue inside the bins.

2Deep Brush Scrubbing

We don't just rely on sprayers. We manually scrub the interior and exterior surfaces with heavy-duty cleaning brushes to break loose stubborn dirt, compacted trash debris, and hidden mold from every corner and crease.

3Thorough Clean Rinse

Once everything is scoured clean, we thoroughly power-rinse the bins inside and out, washing away all loosened debris and grime to leave behind a perfectly clean surface.

4Botanical Deodorizing Spray

To finish the job, we mist the bins with a premium, botanically-derived hospital-grade disinfectant and deodorizer. This specialized formula kills odor-causing bacteria on contact using natural plant oils, leaving your bins completely sanitized and smelling incredibly fresh.
