Carter Hashman

Owner & Operator

Welcome to Bin Bright! My name is Carter Hashman, and I am a student at Ledford Middle School. Alongside managing this business, I am proud to be an honor roll student, a student-athlete, and an active member of multiple school clubs.

I have always been passionate about helping out our local community. By launching Bin Bright, I found a way to provide a much-needed, reliable service to my neighborhood, while also building real-world entrepreneurial experience.

Beyond serving my neighbors, starting this business allows me to take personal responsibility and help ease the financial burdens on my parents for my various extracurricular activities and sports. When you choose Bin Bright, you are supporting hard work, dedication, and local youth entrepreneurship.

Thank you for supporting my business and letting me help keep our neighborhood clean and fresh!
